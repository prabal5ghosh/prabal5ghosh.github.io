01 / ABOUT ME

Working where AI meets biomedical science.

I am a data scientist and AI researcher based in Sophia Antipolis, France. My work connects artificial intelligence, medical imaging, multimodal learning and computational healthcare, with a particular interest in modelling how disease evolves over time.

At Inria Epione, in collaboration with CHU Nice, I investigate longitudinal clinical assessments and quantitative muscle MRI for facioscapulohumeral muscular dystrophy (FSHD). My research explores interpretable, coupled latent ordinary differential equations to connect multiple aspects of disease progression.

Previously, I worked on explainable AI for CNNs at Inria, with an emphasis on concept detection and relevance-based explanations. My background also includes biomedical mathematical modelling, scientific machine learning and professional software engineering at Freshworks.

I earned an MSc in Data Science & Artificial Intelligence from Université Côte d’Azur and an MCA with a University Gold Medal from Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham.
