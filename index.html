About

Researching intelligent systems for biomedical data

I am a Data Scientist and Artificial Intelligence researcher with experience spanning medical imaging, multimodal machine learning, deep learning, explainable artificial intelligence and computational healthcare.

My research experience at INRIA Sophia Antipolis has included MRI analysis for Facioscapulohumeral Muscular Dystrophy (FSHD) as well as explainability and concept detection in convolutional neural networks.

Before focusing on biomedical AI research, I worked at Freshworks as a software engineer, gaining professional experience in backend and full-stack software development using Python, Django, Ruby and Ruby on Rails.

I completed my MSc in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence at Université Côte d'Azur, France.
